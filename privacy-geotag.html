3. Location Data, Map Tiles & Tamper-Proof Time

Because Geotag: GPS Map Camera is a field survey and geotagging utility, location telemetry is central to its operation. We handle location data under strict privacy safeguards:

  • No Background Location Tracking: The Application does not request ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. GPS sensors are active only while the Application is actively open on your screen and stop immediately when the app is closed.
  • Local Watermarking & EXIF Metadata: Coordinates, street addresses, and altitude are drawn directly onto your photo/video bitmap and written into the local file's EXIF tags on your device. You may also lock or customize coordinates manually inside the app.
  • Reverse Geocoding & Static Map Tiles: When an internet connection is available, the Application requests map preview tiles (e.g., OpenStreetMap tile servers) and queries Android's built-in Geocoder to convert latitude/longitude into a human-readable street address for your watermark. No personal identity or user profile is attached to these tile requests.
  • SNTP Time Verification: To provide tamper-proof audit timestamps, the Application queries standard public NTP time servers to compute clock offset locally without transmitting any personal or device identifiers.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep all industry watermark templates, GPS radar tools, and HD photo/video stamping features free for users, the Application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) to serve ads, measure impressions, and prevent fraudulent clicks.
  • Device & Network Information: Device brand, hardware model, OS version, screen dimensions, language, mobile carrier, and coarse IP-based location.
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Banner and interstitial ad impressions, click events, and diagnostic performance metrics.

7. Your Rights & Choices (GDPR & CCPA)

You maintain full ownership and control over your captured media, permissions, and advertising preferences:

  • Revoke Permissions Anytime: You can grant or revoke Location, Camera, or Microphone permissions at any time in your device's Settings > Apps > Geotag: GPS Map Camera > Permissions.
  • Coordinate Lock & Privacy Control: You can lock, hide, or customize address and coordinate fields on your watermark templates before capturing or sharing photos.
  • Manage or Delete Local Captures: All photos, videos, and project entries are stored locally on your device and can be deleted at any time from the in-app Saved Vault or your system Gallery.
  • Reset Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time under Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
