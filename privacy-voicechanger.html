100% On-Device Audio Processing & Zero Cloud Upload Guarantee

Voice Changer processes voice recordings and transforms acoustic pitch, timbre, echo, and frequencies entirely on your local physical device. Your raw voice recordings and exported audio files are NEVER transmitted, uploaded, monitored, or stored on external cloud servers. In compliance with Android OS architecture and Google Play policies, this utility does NOT record, intercept, or modify live cellular phone calls.

Welcome to Voice Changer: Audio Effects (also referred to as "Voice Studio", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and published by Vision AI Labs.

This Privacy Policy details our operational practices concerning audio recording, on-device audio manipulation, device permissions, and advertising identifiers when you use our mobile software.

3. Compliance with Google Play In-Call Recording Policies

In strict accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and Android operating system security guidelines:

  • No Call Recording or Live Call Interception: The Application is NOT a telephone call recorder and does NOT tap, record, or modify active cellular calls or encrypted third-party VoIP calls.
  • Operating System Audio Policy: The Android operating system enforces strict hardware-level audio concurrency locks that silence or block third-party microphone access while a phone call is active. Voice Changer respects all OS-level audio boundaries.
  • Independent Audio Utility: Voice Changer is designed exclusively as an entertainment and audio utility to record audio clips, apply sound filters (e.g. Robot, Alien, Echo, Chipmunk, Helium, Giant), and export creative voice notes.

4. Audio Export, Sharing & Nominative Trademark Notice

Users can save processed recordings to their local app vault or export audio clips (e.g. standard high-fidelity audio notes) to compatible chat and messaging applications via the Android System Share Sheet:

  • Secure Local Transfer: Exported audio files are made available via Android's secure FileProvider mechanism with temporary read permissions granted strictly to the target application chosen by the user.
  • Nominative Fair Use Disclaimer: Any references to third-party messaging applications or platforms—such as WhatsApp, Telegram, or Facebook Messenger—are made strictly for nominative fair-use purposes to describe file format interoperability and sharing destinations.

5. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)

To support free access to sound effects and ongoing development, the Application incorporates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information and device identifiers:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID): Used by Google AdMob to deliver relevant ads and detect fraudulent impressions. Users can reset or delete their advertising identifier at any time in Android OS Settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google's official documentation: Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

7. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area, UK, and California), you possess specific rights regarding personal data:
