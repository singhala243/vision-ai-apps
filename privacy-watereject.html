Privacy Policy for Water Eject: Speaker Cleaner

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

Acoustic Maintenance & Safety Disclosure

Water Eject uses acoustic resonant sound sweeps (165Hz–140Hz) and synchronized haptic vibrations to mechanically displace moisture and dust from mobile speaker grilles. Microphone access is used exclusively in real time for ambient decibel (dB) sound measurement and a 3-second hardware loopback check. Audio data is processed strictly in volatile device memory (RAM) and is NEVER recorded, saved, or uploaded. Keep the device away from your ears while high-volume cleaning cycles are active. This utility does not repair physically broken speaker hardware.

3. Android Permissions & Device Sensors

The Application requests only the minimal set of permissions required to deliver its acoustic functionality. Sensitive permissions are never requested on initial launch and require explicit user consent:

RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone)

Requested strictly on-demand for: (1) Calibrated Decibel (dB) Sound Meter; (2) 3-Second Mic Diagnostic Loopback. Audio is sampled in real-time volatile RAM buffers to calculate Root Mean Square (RMS) amplitudes. Audio recordings are NEVER saved to internal storage, nor uploaded to any external server.

MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS

Allows the app to route acoustic cleaning frequencies between the main bottom speaker and the top call earpiece, and optimize media volume for maximum diaphragm excursion force.

VIBRATE

Controls device haptic motors (VibratorManager) to produce synchronized mechanical vibrations that physically assist acoustic waves in loosening dust, sand, and liquid droplets from speaker grilles.

ACCELEROMETER / GRAVITY SENSOR

Reads device orientation on the emergency prep sheet to advise the user when the phone is positioned face-down, allowing gravity to facilitate moisture evacuation. Sensor readings remain strictly in memory.
