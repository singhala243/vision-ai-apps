100% On-Device Processing & Zero Cloud Upload Guarantee

Video Compressor executes all video transcoding, bitrate compression, resolution downscaling, audio re-encoding, and video cutting/trimming strictly on your local device hardware using Android's native MediaCodec and Media3 engines. Your private videos, recordings, and media clips are NEVER transmitted, uploaded, monitored, or stored on external cloud servers. The app uses the native Android Photo/Video Picker and does not request broad storage permissions.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Video Compressor: Resize Video (referred to as "Video Compressor", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and published by Vision AI Labs.

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining the highest security and transparency standards. This Privacy Policy details our operational practices concerning video compression, local media access, device permissions, and advertising identifiers when you use our mobile software.

2. Permissions & Data Handling

To perform fast, hardware-accelerated video compression without requesting broad storage access, the Application utilizes modern Android system APIs. Below is a comprehensive breakdown of their exact scope and operational behavior:

Permission / API Purpose Storage / Upload Status
Android Photo/Video Picker Allows you to select specific video files for compression. The app cannot see or read your general photo library. 100% On-Device • Zero Storage Permissions
INTERNET Required exclusively by Google AdMob SDK to request and render banner and interstitial advertisements. Used solely for AdMob network requests
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Enables Google AdMob to detect whether an active Wi-Fi or cellular network is available. Device network state only
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) Used to display an ongoing real-time progress notification when transcoding large videos in the background. Local notification display only
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC Maintains the hardware transcoding process if the user switches apps or locks the screen, preventing corrupt output. Local processing lifecycle only
WAKE_LOCK Prevents CPU deep-sleep while hardware encoders are processing frames. Hardware wake state only

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep all video compression, conversion, and editing features completely free for all users, the Application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) to measure ad impressions and prevent fraudulent clicks.
  • Device & Network Information: Device brand, model, operating system version, screen resolution, mobile carrier, and approximate location (coarse IP-based country or city level).
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Impressions, click events, and ad view durations.

For further details on how Google processes and protects advertising data, please consult:

Google Privacy Policy Google AdMob Data Safety Guide

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The Application is designed for general audiences and is not directed towards children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have complete control over advertising personalization and local media files:

  • Reset Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in Android settings under Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
  • Manage Local Videos: All exported compressed videos are saved to your public Movies directory (Movies/VideoCompressor) and can be viewed, shared, or permanently deleted using any file manager or gallery application.
